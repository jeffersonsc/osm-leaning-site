Coherence

measurement "quality",
point by point

0 = garbage. 1 = trustworthy.

Coherence measures how much the sound that reached the mic correlates with the signal the generator sent. Range: 0 to 1, frequency by frequency.

High coherence = you're measuring the SPEAKER.
Low coherence = you're measuring room NOISE, or reflections took over, or the level is too low.

Rule of thumb

If coherence dropped at that frequency, DON'T trust what magnitude and phase show there.

Don't EQ out a notch with coherence of 0.3.

Coherence scale
1.0
Perfect — anechoic chamber
0.95
Excellent — target in real rooms
0.85
Acceptable — bass/extreme highs
0.60
Poor — something interfering
0.20
Garbage — redo setup
Real example: magnitude + coherence on the same screen
⚠ What makes coherence drop
Ambient noise fan, A/C, conversation, traffic
Low level signal must be 20+ dB above noise floor
Strong reflections reverberant room, mic near walls
Wrong setup inadequate FFT, signal lacking energy in that band
Silent speaker frequencies outside the speaker's range
