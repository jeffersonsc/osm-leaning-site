Delay and delay finder

sound takes time to arrive —
and OSM measures it

Sound is not instantaneous.

Sound travels at ~343 m/s in air (20°C). That means every meter of distance = 2.9 milliseconds of delay.

When you start the generator in OSM, the software plays the signal to the output AND records when it played. When the mic captures the sound, OSM compares the two and calculates how long it took — that's the estimated delay.

Delay finder (Cmd/Ctrl + E)

Always apply before storing a measurement.

Without it, phase is offset and comparisons are wrong.
